Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

x_linux is a Telegram-based application that allows users to securely connect their TikTok account through TikTok's official Login Kit and OAuth authorization.

Information We Access

With your permission, the application may access basic TikTok profile information and other information allowed by the permissions you authorize.

How We Use Information

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to provide the features of the x_linux application and to display authorized information to the user.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or publicly share your TikTok information. Information is not used for unrelated purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. Users should never share passwords, client secrets, or access tokens with anyone.

Third-Party Services

The application uses TikTok's official authentication and API services. TikTok's own privacy policies and terms also apply when you use TikTok services.

Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the application owner through the contact information associated with this project.
